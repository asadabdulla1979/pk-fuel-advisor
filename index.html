      How it works. Since 1 July 2026, OGRA sets prices daily off a 7-working-day rolling average of Platts Arab Gulf product assessments (Mogas-92 for petrol, HSD 10ppm for diesel), converted at the interbank rate, plus fixed levies and margins (GST currently zero). This tool advances that 7-working-day window one day per revision — dropping the oldest day and adding the latest Brent spot — rather than projecting the whole crude gap at once, and adds a small per-fuel crack term so petrol and diesel can move apart. Each morning a scheduled task compares the last prediction to the actual OGRA figure, splits the miss into a crude (one-day roll) part and a crack residual, carries a smoothed, decaying crack forward, and scans oil-market news for 2-day/4-day/1-week direction signals. It's an estimate, not the official number.
